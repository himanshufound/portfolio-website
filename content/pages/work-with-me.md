I build for small businesses that need a website that loads fast, looks like it was made for them, and takes some repetitive work off their plate. There is one package, and its price is fixed.

## What you get

- **A fast website.** It has up to 5 pages, works well on phones, loads in under a second on a normal connection, and has basic SEO set up. You get the code and the hosting account in your name.
- **One AI automation.** Pick the job that wastes the most of your time. For example: answer common enquiries from your contact form, sort and summarise incoming emails, turn form submissions into a spreadsheet plus a reply, or draft social posts from your new blog posts.
- **A handover call.** 30 minutes recorded. You'll see how to edit the site and how the automation works.
- **14 days of fixes after launch.** If anything I built breaks, I fix it.

## Timeline

1. **Day 0:** a 20-minute call. We agree on pages, content and the one automation.
2. **Days 1–5:** I build. You get a private preview link to check progress.
3. **Day 6:** one round of changes.
4. **Day 7:** we go live on your domain, then do the handover call.

## Price

**From $600.** That covers everything above. You pay 50% to start and 50% at launch. Bigger sites or extra automations are quoted separately before any work starts.

## Not included

Ongoing content writing, paid ads, logo or brand design, and the costs of third-party tools (domain, hosting above the free tiers, AI API usage). I'll tell you exactly what those come to before we start, and most small sites run on free tiers.
