Until now this site was a business card: who I am, a few projects, a contact link. That works for recruiters. It does nothing for the person who has a problem I've already solved.

So it's turning into a notebook too. Here's what to expect:

- **Build notes.** What I made, how long it took, what it cost to run, and what broke.
- **Small-business automations.** Setups that save a shop or a studio a few hours a week, with the steps to copy them.
- **Tools I actually use.** When I link to a tool, it's because I used it. Some of those links are affiliate links. The [disclosure page](/disclosure) explains how that works.

If you'd rather I just build the thing for you, that's what [work with me](/work-with-me) is for.
