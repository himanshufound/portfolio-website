# Design

## Visual Direction

Personal collage meets motorsport energy, with a soft cherry blossom atmosphere pushed through a sharper, faster frame. The site should feel like a curated sketchbook that happens to be ready for serious work.

## Color

Use a committed palette that blends sakura pink with Ferrari red, anchored by warm near-neutrals and a deep charcoal. Pink can handle softness, red should carry momentum, and the neutral base should keep the page from turning sugary.

Favor tinted neutrals instead of pure white, and keep contrast strong enough for recruiter scanning on bright screens and phones.

## Typography

Use a confident modern sans for most copy, paired with a distinct handwritten or script accent for your name or signature moments. The display treatment should feel personal rather than decorative, with strong weight contrast and clear hierarchy.

## Layout

Build the page as a narrative scroll with a striking hero, a personal intro, a selective project gallery, and a clear contact finish. Use asymmetry, layered media, and varied spacing to avoid template sameness.

## Components

Hero masthead, personal intro panel, project previews, social/contact rail, floating accent marks, and image-forward project cards with lively framing.

## Motion

Use restrained entrance motion and staggered reveals. Motion should make the page feel handcrafted and lively, not busy. Keep it smooth, quick, and easy to ignore if reduced motion is requested.
