slipstream

A pit wall tool that says when to listen, not what the driver feels.

Slipstream scores F1 team radio against each driver’s own baseline and surfaces the calls worth a second listen. It makes exactly one recommendation — how much room the comms channel has — and never rates the driver.

  • voice, car telemetry, and race control cross-checked
  • every number traced back to its weight
  • refuses to answer when the audio is weak
